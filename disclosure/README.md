# Solid Ground: the complete record

*Shannon Dobbs · published October 8, 2026 · text CC BY 4.0, code Apache 2.0*

In December 2025 I filed five patent applications on a way for people and machines to coordinate through shared records without anyone giving up control of their own. In 2026 I let all five go, and I pledged never to assert a patent on any of this, against anyone ([PLEDGE.md](PLEDGE.md)). This folder publishes everything I developed along the way, so that it is public, dated, and open for anyone to read, build on, and improve, and so that nobody can fence it off.

**What changed since v1.0:** see [ERRATA.md](ERRATA.md).

**Start with Volume 1.** Volume 2 is the historical record and carries the most detail on the physical systems (the off-grid node, the food infrastructure, the hubs and the sensory-assist interface). Volume 3 is the most complete technical statement of the coordination method.

**Already running.** The interview that draws out a person's own skill file, the first step of the learning method, is published as the Regenerative Gem at [shannondobbs.com/regenerative-gem](https://shannondobbs.com/regenerative-gem/).
