# Patent non-assertion pledge

*Shannon Dobbs · November 19, 2026 · part of* Solid Ground: the complete record *(https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23251327)*

In December 2025 I filed five United States patent applications on the methodology published in *Solid Ground: the complete record*. I have let all five go, and none of them will become a patent. Application 19/411,766 was abandoned on September 3, 2026. The three provisional applications were not carried forward, and they expire automatically on December 10 and 15, 2026, twelve months after filing. For the last one, application 19/409,604, I filed an express abandonment with the USPTO on November 19, 2026. I am publishing everything instead, and I am making this pledge so that nobody has to take my word for what that means.

**Who makes it.** I, Shannon Dobbs, and Food System Hackers PBC, a Colorado public benefit corporation that does business as Fellowship of Living Systems and that I own. In this pledge, "we" and "us" mean both of us.

**The pledge.** We pledge never to assert any patent or patent application that either of us holds now or obtains later, in any country, against anyone, for making, using, selling, offering for sale, importing, teaching, or otherwise practicing anything disclosed in *Solid Ground: the complete record*, in any of its versions.

To **assert** means to sue or threaten to sue, to demand payment or a license, or to bring any other legal or administrative action, including a counterclaim.

**What it covers.**
- Every patent or application either of us holds now or obtains later, including any patent that might ever issue from the five applications listed below, or from any application claiming their benefit.
- Everything in the record: all versions, all volumes, the reference code, the drawings, and anything either of us adds to the record later.
- Practicing it on its own or as part of a larger product, service, or system, and in modified or improved forms, to the extent that what is practiced is disclosed in the record.
- Everyone: individuals, communities, nonprofits, companies, and governments, for any purpose, commercial or not.

**What it means in practice.** You do not need to ask, notify, or pay either of us to use any of this. The only conditions are those of the licenses: CC BY 4.0 for the text and drawings and Apache 2.0 for the code, which chiefly ask for attribution and the standard license notices.

**No conditions.** This pledge is unconditional, and it is separate from the patent license in Apache 2.0. It does not end if someone sues us.

**How long it lasts.** It is irrevocable. It binds us, our heirs, successors, and assigns, and anyone who acquires or exclusively licenses any of these patents or applications. Any transfer or license of them is made subject to this pledge.

**What it does not do.** It does not give anyone a right to use my name, or the Fellowship of Living Systems or Food System Hackers names, to suggest endorsement. It does not change the attribution the licenses require. It is not a promise about anyone else's patents. It covers only rights we hold now or obtain later.
